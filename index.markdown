Hi! My name is Reece Villarin Akana and I am passionate about bio and AI. I'm currently making a transition into AI safety. I did my PhD in Cancer Biology at Stanford in the [Jerby Lab](https://jerbylab.squarespace.com/) studying genetic regulators of tumor cell sensitivity to CD8+ T cell killing. I then participated in a STEM Fellowship at Anthropic, where I build evals and envs for Claude in bio. While there, I was simultaneously impressed and worried about the future capabilities of AI. Currently my biggest interests are in AI safety and biosafety, and would love to have conversations with anyone in this field!   

In my free time I like to [boulder](https://www.instagram.com/reeceontherocks/) and play [piano](https://on.soundcloud.com/FUCcZmj0OiZbpGBO1p).
